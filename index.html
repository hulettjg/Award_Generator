📖 How to Use This Tool

  1. Upload Photo: Click or drag-and-drop the winner's photo (recommended: 5x7 ratio, 1000x1400px)
  2. Adjust Photo: Use zoom, rotation, and position controls to perfectly frame the subject. You can also drag the photo directly with your mouse.
  3. Configure Award: Select award type, category, time period (year is auto-filled), and winner name
  4. Generate: Choose your output:
    • With Photo: Complete award ready to print
    • Overlay Only: Transparent overlay for Word workflow
    • Photo Unavailable: Standalone award
  5. Save: Right-click → "Copy Image" or "Save Image As"

💡 Pro Tips

  • ✓ Use high-resolution photos (at least 1000x1400px)
  • ✓ Ensure good lighting and contrast
  • ✓ Position subject's head below the top gold bar
  • ✓ Use keyboard arrow keys for fine position adjustments (Shift for larger steps)
  • ✓ Drag the photo directly in the preview for quick positioning
  • ✓ Save as PNG to preserve quality
